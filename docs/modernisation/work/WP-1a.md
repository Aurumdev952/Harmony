1. Check: `baseline.py --compare` between the two runs passes.~~ Replaced by the lead's ruling of 2026-10-05 (option 3 of the quiet-host request): a paired comparison. PERF-7's threshold applies to the paired ratio, candidate p95 over reference p95 per case and per dashboard time to last tile. Absolute numbers and host load are evidence only.
